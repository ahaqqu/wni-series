# F1 — Roads & Traffic Infrastructure (Jakarta / Jabodetabek)

Angle 1 for *WNI Pejuang Nafkah* commute simulation. Focus: lane geometry, arterial names to SCBD/Sudirman, intersections, signals, TransJakarta busway, flyover/underpass, typical speeds, motorcycle filtering, Bintaro–Pondok Aren–Ciputat.

Research date: 2026-10-18. Sources: Wikipedia (road/transit articles), Google Scholar abstracts, Kemenhub via Wikipedia citation, Fatmawati intersection microsim paper (Universitas Indonesia / PKJI 2023).

**Open / weaker points (not closed this pass):**
- Exact **lane counts** (e.g. "3+busway+3") per corridor are well-known operationally but not cleanly stated in the free pages fetched; OSM `lanes=*` tags or Dishub DKI drawings would harden this. Working assumption for 3D: Sudirman / Gatot Subroto / Rasuna Said ≈ **3–4 mixed lanes each way + dedicated busway (1 lane, median or kerbside) + turn pockets at big simpang**.
- Exact **signal cycle lengths** in peak (e.g. 120–180 s) not found in free text this pass; Fatmawati study confirms cycles are long enough that reduction is a major optimisation lever. Use 90–150 s as plausible range, flag as low confidence.
- **Padat merayap vs macet total** speed bands (e.g. 5–15 vs 0–5 km/h) are widely anecdotal; the 14–16 km/h motorcycle figure is congested-arterial, not free-flow. Free-flow on Sudirman off-peak can approach the 50 km/h metro limit.

### Gameplay implications

- Model Sudirman–Thamrin–Gatot Subroto–Rasuna Said as **wide multi-lane arterials with median or kerb busway**, heavy vertical layering (flyover/underpass at Semanggi, Casablanca, Pancoran, JORR ramps) — not flat grids.
- **TransJakarta busway is a separate lane with concrete separators** and centre/island stations: players on motorbikes cannot casually use it; cars blocked by barriers. Station boarding = fixed time sink.
- **Motorcycle filtering is the core micro-decision**: riders squeeze between cars in padat merayap; speed advantage is real (~15 km/h vs near-0 cars) but risky (doorings, sudden lane changes). Cars must anticipate bikes in the "gaps."
- **Signals at major simpang (Fatmawati-like)**: long cycles, queues 200–300 m, delay ~85 s/vehicle is a *good* peak state — worse is common. Right-turn bans and multi-intersection platoons matter for route choice.
- **Ganjil-genap** on Sudirman/Rasuna Said during 06–10 & 16–21 weekdays is a car-routing constraint (plate parity); motorcycles are typically exempt — another bike-vs-car tradeoff.
- **Bintaro/BSD/BSD-corridor players**: toll choice (Ulujami–Serpong, JORR) vs arterial (Jl. Raya Bintaro / Ciputat / Pondok Aren) vs KRL (Pondok Ranji/Jurangmangu/Sudimara). Tolls cost money (JORR ~Rp16k + Serpong toll) but skip lights; arterials hit lights + angkot + filtering chaos.
- Speeds: free-flow arterial ≈ 40–50 km/h (legal cap 50); padat merayap ≈ 10–20 km/h with constant weaving; macet total ≈ 0–5 km/h stop-and-go, motorcycles still advance via filtering.
- Sidewalks + JPO exist on Sudirman but are often blocked/used by parked bikes/vendors — last-100-m walking to SCBD offices is part of the commute feel.

### Visual / spatial notes

**Sudirman corridor (hero set-piece):** Long straight canyon of glass towers (Wisma 46, Intiland, Pacific Place/SCBD edge). MRT vents / station entrances at street level. **Median or near-median TransJakarta lanes** with grey concrete jersey barriers and elevated island stations (glass canopy, blue TransJakarta branding, "INTEGRASI" tags). Painted busway, worn white lane lines, yellow box junctions. JPO pedestrian bridges (Karet Sudirman "Pinisi" bridge is a landmark). Bike lane paint (sometimes green) on the right; often occupied.

**Semanggi:** Iconic multi-level cloverleaf. View from below: Sudirman runs under concrete girders; loop ramps curl overhead; 2017 flyover ramps sweep Cawang→HI and Grogol→Senayan at 6–11.5 m height. TransJakarta Semanggi station sits in the interchange. Night: sodium/LED streetlights, red tail-light rivers, high-rise windows.

**Gatot Subroto:** Parallel stack — surface arterial with median busway (Corridor 9), **Jakarta Inner Ring Road elevated deck above/beside** for much of the length. Parliament (MPR/DPR) complex, Telkom Landmark, SCBD towers on the north-west end. Intersections: wide multi-lane signalised simpang with U-turn underpasses.

**Rasuna Said:** Wide boulevard, **Jabodebek LRT elevated guideway down the centre** (modern concrete viaduct, three stations). Embassies / Mega Kuningan access roads. Casablanca underpass + Satrio flyover as grade-separation set pieces. Busway stations on the sides (Kuningan Timur, Patra Kuningan, etc.).

**Bintaro–Pondok Aren–Ciputat:** Lower-rise, tree-lined estate roads inside Bintaro Jaya (sector gates, roundabouts, modern malls like BXchange), then **arterial squeeze** on Jl. Raya Bintaro / Jl. Ciputat Raya / Jl. Boulevard Bintaro: 2–3 lanes, angkot (share taxis) stopping anywhere, school zones, markets, water-bottle road dividers, occasional flyover at Bintaro Viaduct / toll gates. Toll plazas (multi-booth canopies) as transition to JORR/Serpong toll.

**Markings & materials:** White dashed lane lines (often faded), solid yellow centre lines, yellow box (jangan masuk) at intersections, white stop lines, zebra crossings (faded), concrete jersey barriers (busway), metal guardrails on tolls, asphalt patchwork and utility covers — imperfect surfaces sell the stress.

**Lighting/weather for mood:** Tropical haze, late-afternoon golden glare westbound on Sudirman, sudden downpours that collapse speeds to crawl and flood low points (e.g. toll KM 7 Serpong history).
