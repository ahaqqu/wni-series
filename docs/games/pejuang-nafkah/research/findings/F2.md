## Gameplay implications (max 8)

1. **Angkot random-stop AI** — Angkot agent pulls over anywhere on the right lane (or half-lane) for *turun/naik*, with no signal sometimes; player must brake, *selip* left, or accept delay. Time cost is the punishment, not a crash physics sim.
2. **Motorcycle *selip* as a skill choice** — When traffic is dense, a "filter" action (with fatigue + collision risk + horn stress) is faster but raises near-miss meter; stay-in-lane is safer but slower. Ojek AI uses the same corridor aggressively.
3. **Busway island crossing** — Approaching a Transjakarta halte on a motorcycle/car means dealing with pedestrians crossing to the median island, buses stopping in the dedicated lane, and concrete busway barriers that forbid illegal entry (portal / *sterilisasi*). Wrong-way into busway = high fine risk event.
4. **PKL sidewalk spill** — Near markets/stations, sidewalk clutter pushes pedestrians into the road; player slows for *ibu* carrying food, sudden shop-front parking, and food-carts at lunch spike.
5. **Banjir mode gate** — Flood state (from Pantau Banjir-like layer) blocks underpasses and low segments first: motorcycles must U-turn or push through shallow water (slow + wet screen effect); cars may stall. Commute timer still runs.
6. **Razia / ganjil-genap decision node** — Ahead of a checkpoint, player sees cones/police and must choose: enter the queue (time loss), take a side-street detour (unknown congestion), or risk the restricted lane (fine / hard fail for "already late" career stakes).
7. **Construction squeeze** — Barriers shrink lanes; trucks parked for works create a single-file choke. Emergency vehicle spawn temporarily clears a path if the player (and AI) yield correctly — social reward for *ngalah*.
8. **Social friction stack (emak-emak / lawan arah / sudden putar balik)** — Low-speed unpredictables that punish tailgating and reward buffer distance. Horn / *tolong* cue is a small dignity/stress resource, not a win button.

## Visual / spatial notes

- **Road hierarchy cues:** wide arterials (Jl. Sudirman, Gatot Subroto, Rasuna Said) with **center busway + concrete median**, side lanes for mixed traffic; motorcycle "informal third lane" often forms in the gutter / between cars.
- **Angkot look:** small minibus (Isuzu Elf / Suzuki Carry class), painted trayek colours, door always open-ish; stops near warung, pasar, and gang mouths.
- **Ojol look:** green/other app jackets, phone mounted on handlebar, box for food delivery; they cluster at *pos ojek* and mall drop-offs (pickup lineups on the curb).
- **Transjakarta halte:** raised boarding island / enclosed station in the median (or elevated for Corridor 13), blue-white bus livery, articulated buses on high-demand corridors; pedestrian access via stairs/bridge/JPO — crowded at peak.
- **PKL geometry:** gerobak (carts) and tenda occupy the first 1–2 m of sidewalk/shoulder; smoke, stacked goods, parked motorcycles for customers narrow the carriageway visually.
- **Banjir visuals:** brown water, floating trash, half-submerged motor tires, people walking on the median/centerline; underpasses and under-flyover dips fill first.
- **Construction:** orange/white water-filled barriers, stacked concrete, dirt trucks, temporary steel plates; Transjakarta "temporary station" scaffolds during MRT works are a landmark of the Sudirman–HI corridor.
- **Police layer:** small pos polisi booths, traffic cones + portable barrier for razia, officers in white/grey; ganjil-genap signs with plate symbols.
- **Time-of-day actors:** school dismissal packs of uniforms + parent motorcycles; morning office flood at SCBD/Dukuh Atas; night ojol + angkot reduction.
