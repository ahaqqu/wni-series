**Scope**: stress, fatigue, time poverty, cost burden, safety anxiety, dignity in crowds, weather, lateness culture, mental load, health

## Gameplay implications — map struggles to mechanics (max 10)

1. **Mental Fatigue Meter (Kelelahan Mental)**  
   Accumulates from heat + crowd density + delay minutes + cost stress + news/insult events. High fatigue lowers patience, increases “amuk” (rage) risk events and poor decisions (risky ojek, confrontation). Decay only with rest/sleep — not mid-commute.

2. **Time Poverty Clock**  
   Real-time departure planning: leave too late → miss the only viable train/car; leave early (“tim subuh”) → lose sleep/energy but gain seat chance. Lateness costs: wage cut, HR scolding, dignity hit. Encode the 04:47–05:36 pre-dawn window as a real strategic choice.

3. **Crowd Density & Dignity (Berdesakan)**  
   Car occupancy as a physical state: standing vs sitting, “pepes” compression when >capacity, pickpocket risk, personal-space violation ticks that drain dignity. Platform “war tempat duduk” minigame: race for seats with fall/fight risk.

4. **Daily Budget vs Fare (Gaji UMR)**  
   Transport cost as % of daily wage. Ojek online surge pricing during rain/peak is a temptation trap: faster but eats lunch money. Walking/angkot cheaper but slower/hotter. No “free” good option.

5. **Disruption Cascade Events**  
   Random ops events (KRL dibatalkan, anjlok, pohon tumbang, banjir) force route re-planning under time pressure; multi-hour waits drain fatigue and raise lateness probability. Uncertainty is the point — not just congestion density.

6. **Weather Stack**  
   Heat raises thirst/fatigue; rain raises fare (ojek) and delay; flood blocks routes. Weather does not “reset” — it stacks on existing fatigue.

7. **Moral Load / News Ticker**  
   Ambient news (corruption scandals vs honest grind) as a periodic mood debuff. Optional: player can ignore phone (short calm) or scroll (brief agency + mood hit) — mental load is a resource tradeoff.

8. **Family Obligation Pull**  
   Side goals: antar anak (school drop-off), need to get home for family, send money. Lateness/fatigue conflicts with family presence — guilt meter. Aligns with “memenuhi kebutuhan keluarga” from psychologist quote.

9. **Safety Anxiety on Motor**  
   Motorbike legs: risk of minor accident, pak ogah, aggressive drivers, weather visibility. Fear as slow drain; crash costs medical + time + money all at once.

10. **“Pejuang Nafkah” Run Loop**  
    Multi-day run, not single-trip high score. Success = survive the week (show up, get paid, keep dignity). Fail states are soft (dignity collapse, health crash, quit job) not just “game over.” Honor the identity: struggle is the content.

## Feeling notes for tone / UI copy

**Tone**: empathetic, dry-humor Indonesian everyday voice — not motivational poster, not disaster tourism. Players should feel *recognized*, not pitied.

**Emotional language to preserve (glossed)**:
- **“Tim subuh”** — *dawn team*; badge of people who leave before 5 AM. UI can surface this as a soft identity/status.
- **“War tempat duduk”** — *seat war*; literal fight for a place to sit. Mechanics name, not “rush for seats.”
- **“Kayak pepes”** — *like steamed food wrapped tight in banana leaf*; body-crush metaphor. Use in crowd UI flavor text.
- **“Kelelahan mental”** — *mental fatigue*; not “stress debuff.” Name the meter this.
- **“Rasa tidak berdaya”** — *helplessness*; the emotional core when traffic steals time. End-of-day summary can surface this.
- **“Pejuang nafkah”** — *livelihood fighter*; frame the player avatar as one of these.
- **“Pahit getir”** — *bitter-stinging*; life texture of the city. Menu / loading flavor.
- **“Berdesak-desakan”** — *crushing/crowding together*; sensory, not “high occupancy.”

**UI copy principles**:
- Lateness messages: factual + human (“Telat 12 menit. HRD sudah tanya.” / *12 min late. HR already asked.*) not arcade fail.
- Fatigue high: short, slightly fatalistic lines (“Kaki pegal. Kepala berat. Tinggal 2 stasiun.” / *Legs ache. Head heavy. 2 stations left.*)
- Cost choices: show fare next to remaining daily budget in rupiah — make the tradeoff visible and painful.
- Avoid English “stress meter” labels in primary UI; keep Indonesian emotional terms with optional gloss.
- Dignity events: crowd crush, angry passenger, pickpocket attempt — micro-copy that names the slight (“Bahu orang asing di lehermu.” / *A stranger’s shoulder on your neck.*)

**What NOT to do**:
- Do not romanticize suffering or turn road rage into a fun power fantasy.
- Do not make poverty the only difficulty axis; time + dignity + fatigue matter equally.
- Do not use “NPC crowd” as pure obstruction — they are other pejuang nafkah.
