## Gameplay implications (max 8)

1. **Stock the player's train as JR 205, not a modern metro.** Stainless body + red-yellow KAIC stripe, 4-door cars with one 6-door car in the consist (foldable seats, denser standing). 8/10/12-car lengths change platform fit and "how far you walk to the exit."
2. **Door gameplay = right-side doors at Jurangmangu & Sudirman.** Side platforms, doors open right. Yellow safety line is the hard boundary — crossing it before the train stops is the fail-state risk (real Jurangmangu trackside injuries).
3. **6-door cars are the crush valve.** When density spikes, spawn a 6-door car as the "you can actually get on" car; fewer seats, more standees, wider door bays. This is the authentic JR-205-in-Jakarta detail.
4. **Density ramp is the core loop.** Bintaro/Jurangmangu morning = board easily; Pondok Ranji–Kebayoran = filling; Tanah Abang = interchange surge (Rangkasbitung terminus + Cikarang transfer); Sudirman–Manggarai = berdesakan, door-area packing, "next train?" decisions. Seat ~54/car, standee budget 90–120/car, then soft-cap into crush.
5. **Manggarai is the stress-test hub.** 230k daily transfers, multi-level (elevated Bogor Line vs at-grade Cikarang), 13 tracks. Transfer wrong platform = real time cost. Sudirman is the SCBD alight node with MRT/LRT/Transjakarta spill-out.
6. **Headway feel: 3–5 min peak on core corridors, thinning fast off-peak (up to ~1 h on sparse branches).** Missed train is not fatal at peak; it is fatal off-peak. Delays (lightning on LAA, trackside incident) truncate service and stack the next trains — model as 5–15 min local delay with rare 60–90 min standstills.
7. **Ticketing micro-loop:** tap KMT / e-money (flazz, e-Money, BRIZZI…) or scan QR (C-Access). Fare is progressive Rp 3,000 / 25 km + Rp 1,000 / 10 km — Bintaro→Sudirman is a short cheap hop. Gate queues and "insufficient balance / top-up at FAM" are real friction.
8. **Station furniture is the visual language.** 2020 KAI station name boards (white/blue with code R05/C12), hanging plants at Jurangmangu (green station), old vs new Tanah Abang building, Manggarai's glass-and-steel 3-storey box beside the 1918 heritage shell. These sell "real" without extra systems.

## Visual / spatial notes (train, station, people)

### Train (JR 205 in KAI dress)
- **Exterior:** bare stainless corrugated sides, red stripe + yellow band (KAIC). Destination roller/LED blinds at cab, reprogrammed to Indonesian termini (Tanah Abang, Rangkasbitung, Jakarta Kota, Bogor, Cikarang…). Coupler: Shibata type (Janney adaptor in Indonesia). Pantograph diamond (PS-21) or single-arm (PS-33) on motor cars.
- **Cab face:** classic 1985 JNR flat face with two-pane windshield; later Musashino "marchen" sets have a softer rounded face — use as a rare variant.
- **Doors:** 4 pairs/side, wide commuter aperture (~1.3 m leaf pairs typical of 205); 6-door cars have three double-leaf bays per side. Sliding plug-style leaves, rubber edges, external emergency handles.
- **Interior:** longitudinal orange/brown bench seats along walls, vertical poles at door bays and mid-car, continuous overhead luggage rack and grab rails; hanging straps from ceiling rails (JR style). Ceiling AC vents (two generations of vent pattern). Two-pane windows (upper drops, lower lifts). Priority seats at car ends near doors (marked, often with stickers). 6-door cars: mostly poles + strap zones, fold-up benches.
- **Color notes:** interior plastics warm beige/orange, not clinical white. Floor dark grey with yellow edge near doors.

### Stations (Bintaro → SCBD spine)
- **Jurangmangu:** small, green, residential. Two raised side platforms under simple canopy; garden valley on north approach; tunnel to Bintaro Xchange under the toll viaduct. Post-2024: white modern facade, lift + escalator towers, platform-edge hanging plants. Quiet-feeling except at 06:30–08:30.
- **Pondok Ranji / Kebayoran:** intermediate Tangsel/South Jakarta stops — Kebayoran slightly larger (historic building, market energy); Pondok Ranji more neighborhood. Both side platforms, yellow safety line, blue/white 2020 name boards.
- **Tanah Abang:** the drama station. Six tracks, island platforms, overhead footbridges, retail warungs and market crowds outside (textile market). New 2025 building + older structures. Massive transfer swirl between Rangkasbitung terminus and Cikarang through-services.
- **Manggarai:** monumental, multi-level, glass roof, escalator rivers. Crowd physics: vertical (3rd-floor Bogor platforms ↔ ground Cikarang) + horizontal (long platforms, 12/13-car trains). Confusion + haste is the mood.
- **Sudirman / Dukuh Atas:** canyon of towers (BCA, Wisma 46). Ground-level platforms, short walk to Kendal tunnel (MRT) and LRT skybridge. Office-worker density, air-con jackets, laptop bags. Alighting wave at 08:00–09:00 is the "arrival" set-piece.

### People & crowd
- Morning inbound: office attire (batik, white shirts, blazers), students, online-ride drivers with helmets on platform, street food vendors just outside gates.
- Crush choreography: form queues behind yellow line → alighting first (often blocked) → surge into door bays → packing to pole/strap zones → incremental shove as Tanah Abang/Manggarai boarders add in.
- Sound: bilingual PA ("Kereta menuju… / The train bound for…"), door chime, metal-on-rail squeal, phone speakers, vendor calls outside.
